Can you live on the minimum wage?

From 1 April 2026, New Zealand's minimum wage is $23.95 an hour. We've crunched the numbers on rent, groceries, power and tax in your part of the country. Tell us about your life, and we'll show you whether the maths works, and what has to give if it doesn't.
