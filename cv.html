Graphic Artist, Design Editor

Financial Times London & Hong Kong 1998 - 2007

I joined the Financial Times in London in 1998 as a Graphic Artist, creating editorial graphics, illustrations and page designs for one of the world's most respected newspapers. It was an environment where accuracy, clarity and visual restraint were held to exceptionally high standards, and it fundamentally shaped my approach to editorial design.

In 2003, I relocated to Hong Kong to become Design Editor, Asia, taking responsibility for all editorial design produced by the bureau. The role expanded well beyond newspaper production, encompassing everything from daily pages and ft.com to magazines, special reports and bespoke publications for readers across the Asia-Pacific region. Working across multiple editions, cultures and time zones demanded a collaborative approach and the ability to deliver consistently under tight daily deadlines.

Returning to London in 2006, I rejoined the Graphics Desk with a broader perspective and a greater emphasis on innovation. Alongside day-to-day editorial production, I continued developing new products and visual storytelling approaches inspired by the work undertaken during my time in Hong Kong. The experience of working in two global newsrooms gave me an international perspective that has continued to influence my work throughout my career.
